# Pack 6 Reflection

## What this pack clarified

Production is an independent domain. It consumes an eligible Booking version, owns what must be prepared, preserves the commercial source without owning it, controls internal production timing, supports routing across facilities, and records amendments or cancellations without deleting history.

## Governed clarifications

- Production is created only for Bookings requiring operational fulfilment.
- The minimum lifecycle is Requested, Planned, In Production and Completed.
- Production is location-independent and occurs at a capable Operational Location.
- No separate Production Facility identity exists.
- Required Ready Time is mandatory; Production Start Time is optional.
- Ownership is role-based and may vary by order, line or operational context.

## Deferred concepts

- Additional lifecycle states.
- Additional Production timing milestones.
- Definitive units, conversions, yields, recipes, batch calculations and aggregation catalogues.

## Workflow observations

None recorded. Workflow v2.0 was treated as frozen.

## Lessons

The resolved gates establish a coherent location-independent Production model without adding a Production Facility concept or departmental ownership. Deferred catalogues and milestones are unnecessary for the current structural contract and remain outside the candidate.
