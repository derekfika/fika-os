# FIKA OS

## A shared operating foundation for the whole of FIKA

FIKA OS is a long-term business programme designed to help FIKA grow without allowing operational complexity to grow at the same rate.

It gives us a shared way to describe how FIKA works, preserve what our Legends know and turn that knowledge into consistent ways of working. Over time, this foundation will help every part of FIKA become more connected, more dependable and easier to improve.

FIKA OS does not begin with software. It begins with our Legends, our business knowledge and a shared understanding of what matters.

At every stage, it should reinforce the three values at the heart of FIKA: **Positivity, Wellbeing and Cohesion**.

## 1. Why FIKA OS exists

FIKA has grown through the energy, care and expertise of our Legends. Their ability to respond to different clients, locations and opportunities has created a wealth of knowledge across the business. Every new Service, Event, Mobilisation and operational challenge adds to it.

Much of this knowledge lives with experienced Legends. Some is captured in documents, spreadsheets and existing systems. Some is carried through conversations, habits and local ways of working. This has allowed FIKA to move quickly, but it also means that growth can create more administration, repeated setup and pressure on a small number of Legends who know how everything fits together.

FIKA OS exists to preserve that knowledge and make it reusable.

Its purpose is not to make every part of FIKA identical. Its purpose is to give the business a common foundation so that genuine differences can be supported without repeatedly rebuilding the same understanding.

The ambition is simple: every new Operational Location, Service and capability should benefit from everything FIKA has already learned—and should make the next one easier.

## 2. The problems it is solving

FIKA OS addresses a connected set of business challenges:

- Important knowledge can be held in individual teams, documents or systems rather than being available as shared company knowledge.
- The same fact may be entered, interpreted or maintained in several places.
- Similar processes can develop different language and rules, even when they represent the same business activity.
- Teams may spend time reconciling information, re-keying details or working out which version is current.
- A change of supplier or system can appear to change the business process because the technology and the business meaning have become too closely linked.
- Opening a new location or introducing a new capability can require repeated setup that should become easier with experience.
- History can be difficult to preserve when names, schedules, responsibilities or operating arrangements change.

None of these issues reflects a lack of effort or expertise. They are natural consequences of a growing business whose systems and processes have evolved at different times to solve immediate needs.

FIKA OS creates the conditions for those local successes to become shared FIKA strength. It brings a positive focus to improvement, reduces avoidable pressure on our Legends and helps separate teams operate as one connected business.

## 3. People First: Positivity, Wellbeing and Cohesion

The defining principle of FIKA OS is:

> Technology should adapt to the way our people work.  
> Our people should never have to adapt to the technology.

At FIKA, our people are our Legends. People First means understanding how our Legends actually deliver great work before changing the tools around them. It does not mean preserving every manual step forever. It means improving work with care and purpose.

The Legends closest to the work understand the decisions, exceptions, pressures and relationships that make an operation successful. FIKA OS treats that experience as valuable business knowledge, not as an obstacle to progress.

Where a process is stable and understood, technology can remove repetition and make the work easier. Where judgement is required, the right information should be presented clearly so that an authorised Legend can make the decision. Responsibility should remain visible, and important actions should remain explainable.

People First also means protecting continuity. A Legend should not need to know which spreadsheet, inbox or individual holds the answer before they can act confidently. FIKA should retain its knowledge when roles change, teams grow or new Operational Locations open.

Our three core values provide the test:

- **Positivity:** focus technology on removing obstacles, helping Legends find answers and creating more time for brilliant work.
- **Wellbeing:** reduce avoidable pressure, repeated admin and dependence on individual memory while giving Legends greater clarity and confidence.
- **Cohesion:** connect departments through shared language and trusted knowledge while respecting the expertise and responsibility of each team.

The result should be less friction, clearer ownership and more time for the work that creates value for clients, customers and fellow Legends.

## 4. How FIKA works today

FIKA already operates through a rich combination of experienced Legends, established processes and practical tools. Teams use spreadsheets, email, calendars, shared documents, dashboards and specialist systems to manage Bookings, Hospitality, Production, Events, workforce information, reporting and other operational work.

These tools were created or adopted to meet real needs. Many remain important to daily operations and contain valuable evidence about how FIKA works.

The challenge is that they do not always share the same language or the same view of the business. Information can move between a Booking request, a dashboard, a calendar entry, a Production view and a report. At each step, Legends may need to interpret, repeat or reconstruct details that FIKA already knows.

Different locations may also have separate versions of similar tools. Some differences reflect genuine client or operational needs. Others reflect the way the tools grew over time. FIKA OS does not assume that all differences should disappear. It first asks which differences matter to the business and which can be managed through shared rules and local choices.

This approach respects the work our Legends have already done. Existing systems are evidence and transition partners, not problems to be discarded. Change should be gradual, measured and safe.

## 5. How FIKA OS is different

Traditional technology projects can begin with a system and then ask the business to fit its work into that system. FIKA OS reverses that order.

It begins by bringing the right Legends together and asking:

- What does this business concept mean to FIKA?
- Who owns that meaning?
- Which relationships and responsibilities must be preserved?
- What changes over time, and what history must remain visible?
- Which differences are genuine, and which are repeated versions of the same idea?
- What should remain true even if the technology changes?

The approach is disciplined: agree business language before choosing tools; preserve history rather than overwrite it; make relationships clear; give each concept one responsibility; and reuse or extend what FIKA already understands before inventing something new. A convenient local shortcut should never create a competing version of FIKA's truth.

Only when those questions are answered does the programme define what future tools must support.

This protects FIKA from allowing a spreadsheet layout, a supplier's terminology or the design of an existing system to become our permanent definition of how the business works.

It also creates reuse. For example, an Operational Location keeps the same business identity even when its name, services or enabled capabilities change. A Booking preserves commercial and service intent, while Production owns what must be prepared. An Event remains distinct from a recurring Service, even when both involve the same Operational Location, Legends or contacts. These clear boundaries let different teams work from shared facts without losing their own responsibilities.

## 6. How the programme works

The programme follows a deliberate sequence:

**Business Discovery**  
We listen to the Legends who understand the work, examine real examples and identify the questions that must be answered.

**Shared Business Language**  
We agree what important terms mean across FIKA. Similar ideas are brought together; genuinely different ideas remain separate.

**Business Canon**  
Approved decisions are preserved as FIKA's shared business authority. The Canon becomes our organisational memory: it records what FIKA means, why key decisions were made and who has authority over them.

**Software**  
Future tools follow the approved business meaning. They do not invent it or redefine it for their own convenience.

In short:

**Business Discovery → Shared Business Language → Business Canon → Software**

This sequence matters. It means that technology follows the business, departments remain the experts in their own work and future change can be governed without losing history.

Business discovery is not a one-off exercise. FIKA will continue to learn. New evidence may show that a decision needs to change, but change will be recorded openly rather than silently overwriting the past.

## 7. Current progress

FIKA OS has moved from an initial idea to a governed business foundation shaped by FIKA's own knowledge and language.

The programme has completed the following work:

- **Vision and scope:** FIKA has defined why the programme exists, what belongs within it and the enduring principles that should guide future decisions.
- **Business landscape:** The major areas of the business, their relationships and the current flow of information have been documented without allowing existing tools to dictate business meaning.
- **Business discovery:** Fifty-four business questions were resolved into approved decisions, with no remaining questions or unresolved contradictions in that discovery cycle.
- **Decision records:** Important decisions have been preserved with their context, rationale, ownership and consequences so that future teams can understand both what was decided and why.
- **Governed knowledge packs:** Eight connected packs now cover foundational identity and locations; capabilities, configuration and authority; Services; Bookings; Events; Production; Mobilisation; Brand; and Waste.
- **Consistency checks:** The governed material has been checked for alignment, traceability and safe use as a shared foundation for later work.

This progress has already clarified several important principles:

- Business ownership, authority, responsibility and system administration are different things.
- Roles and authority should be explicit and auditable rather than dependent on named individuals or assumed seniority.
- A location's identity is separate from the services and capabilities available there.
- Recurring Services, individual Bookings, bespoke Events and Production work are related but distinct business concepts.
- A Booking records commercial and service intent; Production records the work required to fulfil it.
- Mobilisation preserves the governed history of establishing or materially changing an operating scope.
- Brand assurance records what was checked, while approval records who authorised a variation.
- Waste is owned as operational business knowledge, while reporting consumes that knowledge without redefining it.

This is a significant foundation, but it is not a claim that every future tool has already been built. It means FIKA now has a clearer and more reusable basis on which those tools can be designed—with Positivity, Wellbeing and Cohesion built into the way decisions are made.

## 8. Department involvement

FIKA OS belongs to every part of FIKA. No central team can define it alone.

Each department contributes the knowledge only it can provide:

- **Legends in Senior Leadership** connect the programme to FIKA's direction, priorities and standards of accountability.
- **Operations Legends** bring knowledge of Operational Locations, capabilities, Services and day-to-day operating responsibilities.
- **Marketing Legends** protect Brand meaning, standards, variations and the quality of how FIKA communicates.
- **Coffee Leadership Legends** contribute specialist knowledge about coffee operations, service expectations, equipment and quality.
- **Hospitality Legends** bring the customer, Booking, service and fulfilment knowledge required at each Operational Location.
- **Production Legends** explain what must be prepared, when it must be ready and how changes affect Production work.
- **Logistics Legends** contribute the knowledge required to move goods, equipment and information reliably between the places that need them.
- **Finance Legends** bring commercial, charging, tax, control and reporting knowledge within their authority.
- **HR Legends** bring workforce, privacy, responsibility and employment-process knowledge within their authority.
- **Site Manager Legends** bring the practical context of local operations, exceptions, client relationships and what teams need to act confidently.

The principle is that FIKA OS is built **with departments, not for departments**.

Our departments and their Legends remain the experts in their work. The programme helps connect that knowledge, expose dependencies and make approved decisions reusable across FIKA. It does not remove departmental judgement or transfer business authority to technology.

## 9. Future vision

Success will not be measured by the number of systems introduced. It will be measured by how much easier it becomes for FIKA to operate, learn and grow.

In a successful FIKA OS future:

- Important knowledge is preserved as company knowledge rather than depending on memory or local files.
- Legends across departments use the same language for the same business concepts.
- Information is entered once wherever practical and reused by the teams that need it.
- Each team can see the current facts, its own responsibilities and the next action required.
- New locations and capabilities build on shared foundations instead of repeating earlier setup.
- Genuine local, client and Brand differences are supported without creating separate versions of business truth.
- Changes retain their history, making decisions easier to explain and review.
- Reporting draws from trusted operational knowledge rather than becoming a competing version of events.
- Technology suppliers can change without forcing FIKA to redefine its business.
- Automation removes understood repetition while keeping authority, exceptions and accountability visible.
- Improvement becomes continuous because new evidence can be added to the same governed learning process.

The long-term result is a more positive, healthy and connected way of working: better knowledge preservation, stronger shared understanding, less duplication, higher quality and more confident decision-making.

Applications may change. Technology may change. The business meaning FIKA has agreed should remain stable, understandable and reusable.

## 10. Invitation

FIKA OS can only represent FIKA accurately if the Legends who know the business contribute to it.

Every department is invited to share the language, decisions, exceptions, relationships and practical knowledge that make its work successful. This includes identifying where information is repeated, where ownership is unclear, where a process relies on individual memory and where a better shared understanding would remove friction.

Contribution does not require technical knowledge. The most valuable input is the real experience of our Legends:

- What are we trying to achieve?
- What do we mean by this term?
- Who is accountable?
- What information is needed to make the right decision?
- What must remain true when circumstances change?
- What history must we preserve?

By answering these questions together, FIKA can express its knowledge once and allow every future process and tool to benefit from it.

FIKA OS exists to help FIKA grow without proportional increases in operational complexity—by putting our Legends first, preserving business knowledge and turning shared understanding into lasting organisational strength.

That is FIKA OS: **Positivity in how we improve, Wellbeing in how we support our Legends and Cohesion in how we grow together.**
