# Stage 3 — Business Discovery

## Purpose

Resolve evidence-backed, dependency-ordered business questions into human-approved canonical decisions.

## Business outcome

FIKA has an approved business-language foundation that later records, schemas, architecture and implementation must follow.

## Inputs

- Stage 2 evidence, domain discoveries, journeys and workshops
- Dependency-driven FIKA Business Knowledge Workbook
- Human answers and approvals from the accountable decision owners

## Core activities

- Ask one decision question at a time in dependency order.
- Prefer evidence-backed suggested answers.
- Record human wording exactly.
- Detect duplicates and contradictions.
- Reopen blocked questions only after all prerequisites are approved.
- Retain provenance and processing history.

## Required artefacts

- FIKA Business Knowledge Workbook
- Questions, decisions, glossary and activity log
- Dependency graph and completion dashboard

## Exit criteria

- Every discovery question is approved and processed.
- No question remains blocked or awaiting a decision owner.
- No duplicate decision exists.
- Contradictions are resolved or explicitly marked for review.
- The decision register is complete and traceable.

## Current status

**Complete.** The workbook contains **54 canonical decisions**, **100% discovery**, **0 remaining questions**, **0 Needs Review items** and **0 duplicate decisions**.

## Dependencies on earlier stages

- Stage 1 — Vision
- Stage 2 — Domain Discovery

## Outputs consumed by later stages

Stage 4 converts the canonical decisions into durable Business Decision Records. All later stages must implement rather than redefine them.

## Out of scope

- Writing Business Decision Records
- Creating or adopting schemas
- Choosing platform architecture
- Implementing or deploying applications
