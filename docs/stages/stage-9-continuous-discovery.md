# Stage 9 — Continuous Discovery

## Purpose

Govern new evidence, questions and change after rollout so FIKA OS can evolve without silently fragmenting business meaning.

## Business outcome

Operational learning improves the platform through traceable decisions rather than undocumented exceptions or copied behaviour.

## Inputs

- Operational feedback and measured outcomes
- Incidents, exceptions and support evidence
- New clients, Operational Locations, capabilities and regulatory needs
- Proposed changes from domain owners

## Core activities

- Capture evidence without immediately changing canonical meaning.
- Identify whether a new question, validation or BDR amendment is required.
- Detect contradiction and duplication.
- Approve changes through accountable human owners.
- Propagate approved changes through BDRs, schemas, architecture, implementation and validation.

## Required artefacts

- New evidence and discovery questions
- New or superseding BDRs
- Impact assessment and traceability updates
- Revised downstream artefacts where approved

## Exit criteria

Continuous discovery has no final closure. Each individual change closes when its decision, downstream updates and validation are complete and traceable.

## Current status

**Planned as an ongoing governance stage.** It becomes active when new evidence or change requests arise.

## Dependencies on earlier stages

- All earlier stages; new change may loop back to the earliest stage affected.

## Outputs consumed by later stages

Approved changes re-enter Stages 4–8 as appropriate. Material changes to purpose or scope return to Stage 1.

## Out of scope

- Silent changes to business meaning
- Treating application behaviour as automatic business authority
- Skipping BDR, schema, architecture or validation impact where relevant
- Using AI as the decision owner
