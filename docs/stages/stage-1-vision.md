# Stage 1 — Vision

## Purpose

Define why FIKA OS exists, the operating problems it should solve and the principles that govern every later decision.

## Business outcome

FIKA has a shared, durable explanation of the platform's purpose: growth without proportional increases in manual work, duplicated knowledge or setup complexity.

## Inputs

- Confirmed FIKA Platform scope
- Existing operating-system evidence and Stage 1 foundation work
- Leadership objectives and operational friction

## Core activities

- Define the platform vision and intended business outcomes.
- Establish scope and explicit exclusions.
- Agree enduring design and operating principles.
- Identify success measures without choosing implementation technology.

## Required artefacts

- Platform scope
- Platform principles
- Vision and success measures
- High-level roadmap

## Exit criteria

- The purpose and boundaries of FIKA OS are understandable to business and technical readers.
- Principles exist for authority, growth, automation, security, user experience and gradual migration.
- Excluded work is explicit.

## Current status

**Complete.** The vision, scope and platform principles are established.

## Dependencies on earlier stages

None. This is the foundation of the staged lifecycle.

## Outputs consumed by later stages

Every later stage uses the vision, scope and principles as decision tests.

## Out of scope

- Domain modelling
- Business-question resolution
- Schemas
- Architecture or technology selection
- Application implementation
