# FIKA OS Cohesion Principles

## Purpose

These principles are the stable philosophical foundation of FIKA OS. They guide business discovery, decisions, schemas, architecture and applications without prescribing technology.

The Canon organises enduring cross-cutting guidance; approved [Business Decision Records](../business-decisions/README.md) remain authoritative for specific business rules.

## Principles

### 1. Build for tomorrow's FIKA.

Choose foundations that let FIKA grow, change and add Operational Locations or capabilities without proportional increases in complexity.

### 2. Business language precedes implementation.

Agree what FIKA means before software, providers or data structures express it.

### 3. Automate only when authorised, tested and trusted.

Automation must have clear authority, proven behaviour and a safe operational boundary before it acts for FIKA.

### 4. History is preserved, not overwritten.

Retain prior meaning, relationships and effective states so change remains explainable and auditable.

### 5. Relationships are modelled explicitly.

Represent how independent business concepts relate instead of hiding relationships inside names, assumptions or duplicated records.

### 6. Capabilities unlock behaviour.

Enable reusable business functions through governed Operational Capabilities rather than redefining the identity or type of an Operational Location.

### 7. Minimise friction.

Reduce avoidable effort for customers, Legends and operational teams while preserving necessary control.

### 8. Cohesion over convenience.

Prefer a consistent whole-platform meaning over a locally convenient shortcut that creates competing truth.

### 9. One concept owns one responsibility.

Give each business concept a clear purpose and owner instead of making one record responsible for unrelated domains.

### 10. Discover before designing.

Use evidence and human business decisions to resolve meaning before proposing schemas, architecture or applications.

### 11. Operational truth precedes software truth.

Software must express how FIKA actually operates and must not turn current screens, providers or storage layouts into business authority.

### 12. Prefer extension over proliferation.

Extend stable concepts through governed relationships, capabilities and configuration before creating another competing concept.

### 13. Every decision should reinforce Positivity, Wellbeing and Cohesion.

Judge decisions by whether they support constructive relationships, healthier work and a more connected FIKA.
