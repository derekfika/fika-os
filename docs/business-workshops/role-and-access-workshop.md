# Role and Access Business Workshop

## Purpose

This workshop confirms business responsibilities, homepage needs, site/project scope and approval authority before any technical access design. It uses plain-English access language and does not grant access.

Status values: `Open`, `Confirmed evidence`, `Recommended`, `Needs evidence`, `Not applicable`.

## Workshop completion gate

The business role map is ready for a decision record only when:

- positive responsibilities are confirmed for each person/role;
- domain actions are separated into view, contribute, manage, approve, publish and administer;
- site/project scope and temporary-assignment rules are agreed;
- sensitive-data restrictions are approved by HR/Finance/Security owners;
- mobilisation task ownership and launch approval are confirmed;
- leadership summary requirements and emergency-access governance are agreed.
