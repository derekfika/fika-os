# Pack 7 Reflection

## What this pack clarified

Mobilisation is one governed programme for establishment, material change or re-establishment of an approved operating scope. It has one accountable role, explicit AUTHMOD authority, a nominated coordinator, classified tasks, readiness evidence, an effective period, an outcome and preserved history.

## Governed clarifications

- Accountability belongs to one organisational role; collective authority is represented through explicit assignments and grants.
- Client, contract and commercial-agreement relationships are optional.
- One Operational Location may have multiple Mobilisation records over time.

## Deferred concepts

- The future canonical phase catalogue; MNK phase families remain workshop evidence only.
- Definitive mandatory-task and readiness-evidence catalogues.
- Exact material-remobilisation threshold.

## Workflow observations

None recorded. The current Stage 5 workflow was followed without redesign.

## Lessons

The resolved gates produce a reusable Mobilisation aggregate without turning management-group membership, Client contracts or the MNK plan into mandatory business structure.
