# Human Decision Resolution

## Status

Resolved by Derek on 2026-07-15. No Pack 7 human decision gate remains open.

## PACK7-GATE-001 — Resolved

- Each Mobilisation has one explicitly accountable organisational role.
- Additional authority uses explicit role assignments and AUTHMOD authority grants.
- Delegation is scoped and effective-dated and does not transfer accountability.
- Seniority, job title or management-group membership does not grant authority.

## PACK7-GATE-002 — Resolved

- Mobilisation is not necessarily Client-contractual.
- Client, contract and commercial-agreement references are optional.
- Internal Mobilisations, capability launches, remobilisations and material operating-model changes are valid.
- No separate Client Mobilisation concept is introduced.

## PACK7-GATE-003 — Resolved

- One Operational Location may have multiple Mobilisation records over time.
- Each record represents one distinct governed programme and preserves its own history.
- Later records reference the same Operational Location without overwriting earlier records.
- Routine changes require a governed materiality decision before creating a Mobilisation.
- The exact material-remobilisation threshold remains deferred.

## Processing consequence

The Mobilisation and readiness schemas, fixtures, traceability and reports have been revised and revalidated. MNK phase names remain workshop evidence only.
