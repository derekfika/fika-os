# Pack 8 Reflection

## What this Pack clarified

- Brand and Waste share a processing Pack only; they are independent domains.
- BRAND-001 supports a narrow Brand Variation record and a separate Brand Assurance Record rather than a new asset, Media or application aggregate.
- WASTE-001 establishes Waste as an Operations-owned domain and provides the minimum concepts of event, catalogue-referenced quantity, reason, Operational Location and immediate Waste Disposition.
- Reporting consumes Waste facts and must not define their canonical meaning.

## Governed clarifications

- Brand Assurance records what was verified against the applicable Brand Standard; approval records who authorised the variation.
- Waste measurement units reference the Operations-owned Measurement Catalogue.
- Waste Disposition is the immediate operational outcome; Improvement Action is a separate later business change.

## Deferred concepts

- Measurement Catalogue values.
- Detailed Improvement Action domain.
- Detailed Brand assurance checklist and Brand asset/Media catalogues.
- Waste reason catalogue, source-domain relationships, correction policy and reporting metrics.

## Workflow observations

None recorded. The frozen Stage 5 workflow was followed.

## Lessons

A BDR can establish a domain without supplying every rule needed for a reliable canonical contract. Structural drafting is useful for exposing those exact gaps, but schema convenience cannot resolve them.
