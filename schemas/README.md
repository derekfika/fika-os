# Schemas

Canonical schemas are a Stage 5 deliverable. They implement accepted Business Decision Records and must not invent business meaning.

## Current status

- Stage 5 has started with the [Pack 1 Draft schemas](pack-1/README.md).
- Pack 1 schemas compile Accepted and Frozen BDRs but remain **Draft and not adopted** pending human schema review.
- Existing `FikaBooking` schemas and fixtures remain draft evidence, not adopted contracts.
- No schema becomes authoritative until its BDR traceability, ownership, validation, fixtures and adoption review are complete.

See [Stage 5 — Schema Design](../docs/stages/stage-5-schema-design.md) and [documentation governance](../docs/documentation-governance.md).
