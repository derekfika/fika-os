# 2026-11-18 — Publish scoped Studio menu amendments

Studio applies a selected section or rotation week to the last published menu. A dish amendment advances that dish across its existing placements. A dish shared with untouched placements keeps its frozen revision during a section or week amendment. The transaction rechecks the exact releases for retained dishes. Each package records its scope and prior publication. Booking impact queries use the relevant catalogue mappings and published rotation anchor.
